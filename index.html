Our Story

Rooted in Bhagalpur, Built for the World

I started Vexora Studio a few years ago from a small room in Tilkamanjhi — my cousin's old laptop, a cracked phone screen for testing, and a conviction that Bhagalpur's businesses deserved the same premium digital presence that large-city companies were getting. That first website I built for a local garments shop — the owner cried when he saw it live. That moment is why I do this.

Since then, Vexora has grown into a focused studio that combines modern web development, AI-powered marketing, and an obsession with performance. I've worked with startups, local shops, coaching institutes, and service businesses — each project treated with the same premium attention regardless of budget. The luxury-tech aesthetic isn't just a style choice; it signals that your business is serious and your digital presence means something.

Every project you bring to Vexora is handled personally by me. You get direct communication, honest timelines, and a website that actually works for you — not just something that looks nice in a presentation. If you're in Bhagalpur or anywhere in Bihar, let's meet on WhatsApp and get started today.
